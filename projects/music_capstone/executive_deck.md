# Fair Artist Discovery Executive Deck

## Slide 1 — Title
Fair Artist Discovery & Recommendation System

## Slide 2 — Business Problem
Major music platforms often over-index on historical popularity. That creates a recommendation loop in which already successful artists receive more exposure, while emerging or niche artists are systematically under-discovered.

## Slide 3 — Why It Matters
- artists lose monetization and discoverability
- listeners see less creative variety
- niche genres remain hidden
- platform trust and fairness are at risk

## Slide 4 — Research Grounding
The project is grounded in the repository research around recommendation bias, long-tail artist discovery, and fairness-aware ML in the music industry.

## Slide 5 — Project Objective
Build a recommendation system that improves engagement while increasing exposure for underrepresented artists.

## Slide 6 — Data and Methods
- structured artist and listener dataset
- cleaning and feature engineering
- descriptive statistics
- classification and regression models
- recommendation ranking with fairness adjustment

## Slide 7 — Key Findings
The analysis shows that a small number of artists can dominate listens and playlist exposure. Without intervention, a recommendation model may reinforce this concentration.

## Slide 8 — Recommendation Strategy
- personalize for listeners
- preserve variety in recommendation lists
- give long-tail artists a meaningful exposure boost
- include fairness metrics in evaluation

## Slide 9 — Technical Stack
Python, pandas, matplotlib, scikit-learn, NumPy, SQL, and ML workflow thinking

## Slide 10 — Business Impact
The platform can increase discovery without sacrificing engagement, creating a stronger product story and a more sustainable artist ecosystem.

## Slide 11 — Next Steps
- expand to real music data
- deploy and monitor the model
- test in live A/B experiments
- integrate artist fairness and diversity scorecards

## Slide 12 — Closing
This project demonstrates a realistic strategy for using AI to improve music discovery while making the platform healthier, fairer, and more scalable.
